# Professor-Facing Quality Gates

Run these checks before finalizing any output.

## 1. Evidence integrity
- Every important achievement is supported by a source or user confirmation.
- No authorship role is inferred from author order.
- No proposed methodology is written as a completed experiment.
- No publication type/status is upgraded.
- Quantities, dates, titles, venues, and institutional names match the evidence.

## 2. Research positioning
- The opening answers: Who is this researcher now?
- The document presents a coherent trajectory, not a list of unrelated experiences.
- Current doctoral research is more prominent than older unrelated work unless the target professor makes the older work relevant.
- Transfer need is not the central narrative.

## 3. Target specificity
- At least two alignment claims are tied to specific professor/lab work.
- Complementarity is distinguished from overlap.
- Proposed joint directions are plausible and clearly labeled as possibilities.
- Fit caveats are retained rather than hidden.

## 4. Tone
- Professional, academically credible, concise.
- No exaggerated praise, desperation, grievance, or negative discussion of prior supervisors.
- Avoid unsupported self-labels such as "expert" or "pioneer".
- Use precise methodological vocabulary when supported.

## 5. Privacy
- Remove birth date, marital status, home address, and personal phone numbers by default.
- Include only professional links and contact details needed for the deliverable.

## 6. Final consistency
- 1-page profile and detailed dossier tell the same research story.
- Email claims match the attached profile.
- Research links are correct.
- If source access was incomplete, disclose the limitation.
