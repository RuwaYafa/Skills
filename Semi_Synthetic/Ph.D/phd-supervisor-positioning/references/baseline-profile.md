## Current doctoral agenda — Evidence level A for proposal content

The pre-proposal frames Arabic LLM evaluation as a methodological measurement problem involving benchmark opacity, controlled data construction, and evaluator/ranking sensitivity.

### Main research lines

1. **Benchmark and leaderboard methodology audit (RQ1)**
   Audit Arabic benchmark/leaderboard design, including task composition, dialect stratification, data provenance, aggregation, comparability, and contamination-related concerns.

2. **Controllable Arabic benchmark synthesis (RQ2)**
   Develop a modular seven-layer lifecycle for benchmark construction with Arabic-specific controls for morphology, diglossia/register, dialects, cultural grounding, validation, and decontamination.

3. **Evaluation methods and stability (RQ3)**
   Compare lexical, semantic, judge-based, and hybrid evaluation methods against expert/expert-proxy judgments and develop a stability auditing protocol for prompt, decoding, judge, and aggregation perturbations.

- Structured audit template for Arabic benchmarks and leaderboards.
- Proposed representational stability metric under morphological and dialectal perturbations.
