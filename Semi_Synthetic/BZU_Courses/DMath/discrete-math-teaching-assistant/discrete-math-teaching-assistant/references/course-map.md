# COMP233 Discrete Mathematics course map

## Governing course intent

The course introduces discrete mathematical ideas and techniques widely used in computer science and related computing programs. Its core objectives are to help students understand logical statements and avoid logical errors, prove mathematical arguments, and apply mathematics in programming through topics such as counting, sets, and induction.

The planned class meeting length is 110 minutes.
